# Welcome to think

Think out loud with an AI agent on an endless canvas. Every block is a file in
this canvas's folder: the agent in the chat edits them with you, and every
change lands in History.

### Try asking the agent

- “Add three ideas for a team offsite”
- “Turn *How it works* into a checklist”
- “Address the comments”

### Find your way

- **Enter** edits a block, **Esc** goes back
- **M** · **D** · **H** · **I** add Markdown, Mermaid, HTML or an image
- Drag from the dots on a block's side to connect it
- **⌘K** (Ctrl K) jumps anywhere · **?** shows every shortcut

### Also good to know

- **History** in the side panel keeps every change: look back, compare, restore
- Agents in your terminal can join in with the `think` command
